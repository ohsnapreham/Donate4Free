How Donate4Free Works

Raising Awareness

Donate4Free raises awareness of charitable causes that support children and refugees.

Give With Your Time

Donate4Free was built around a simple idea. Ad revenue from YouTube videos could fund charitable donations, giving viewers a way to help without spending money.

Feature Independent Creators

Donate4Free features selected community videos and credits the creators so their work can reach a wider audience.

About Donate4Free

Our Mission

Why Donate4Free Started

In 2020, a widely shared video about childhood malnutrition prompted a question. How could people help if they could not afford to donate? Donate4Free was created to give them a way to contribute their time by watching videos.

The YouTube Project

Donate4Free launched a YouTube channel with the goal of donating all of its ad revenue to charity. The channel reached more than 1,000 subscribers and qualified for monetization within its first month.

What’s Next

A future goal is to build an application that can generate additional revenue for donations. Donate4Free is interested in hearing from people who can help with software development, financial oversight, or social media.

Videos

The channel features vlogs, short videos about interesting facts, and occasional submissions from viewers. Contributors receive credit when their work is featured.
