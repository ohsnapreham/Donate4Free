About Donate4Free

In 2020, a widely shared video about childhood malnutrition prompted a question. How could people help if they couldn’t afford to donate?

Donate4Free began as a YouTube channel built around that question. It reached more than 1,000 subscribers and qualified for monetization within its first month.

A future goal is to build an application that can generate additional revenue for donations. If you’re interested in helping with software development, financial oversight, or social media, get in touch.
